A gaming stream or gaming video (any game). Viewers on Shorts/TikTok/Reels stop for
strong emotion, surprise and personality, not for the game itself.

Categories (use these as "kind"):
- reaction: an insane reaction, losing it, screaming, speechless, jumping out of the chair
- clutch: an impossible play, a comeback, a last-second win, a crazy skill moment
- funny: a fail, a glitch, a chaotic moment, a roast, a bit people send to a friend
- discovery: they find or notice something hidden, unexpected or mind-blowing
- rage: a rage moment, a meltdown, blaming the game, a controller-throwing loss
- take: a strong opinion people will argue about in the comments
- story: a short story or explanation that is interesting on its own

Hashtags: the game name (if you can tell it) and the streamer's or channel's name first.
One broad tag like #gaming at most. Never invent a game name you can't tell from the
title or the transcript.
